Hypnotherapy

Start with one real situation.

You may understand what you want and still find yourself hesitating, overthinking or repeating a familiar response. A session offers space to explore that experience through conversation, focused attention and imagery.

Hypnotherapy

What hypnosis means here.

Guided attention and imagination can help you explore your own responses. Systemic mapping or work with inner parts may also be discussed. You remain a participant: ask questions and choose whether to continue.

A shared process

How sessions work

The approach is chosen together. You do not need to use every method.

  1. 01

    Map your question

    Start with your goal, the situation around it and the boundaries that matter to you.

  2. 02

    Explore the pattern

    Notice competing needs, roles and perspectives through conversation and systemic mapping.

  3. 03

    Work experientially

    Discuss hypnosis, parts work or guided imagery when they fit your question.

  4. 04

    Integrate

    Reflect on the experience and choose a practical next step. Symbolic work is an optional addition.

Before you decide

A few practical questions.

Will I have a say in the process?

Yes. Discuss the approach before starting, ask questions and choose whether to participate. You can ask to pause or stop at any time.

How many sessions will I need?

There is no promised number of sessions or guaranteed result. Start by discussing your question and whether this approach is a suitable fit.

Can we meet online?

Mention your preferred format in your enquiry. Confirm availability, duration and fees with Andrey before booking.

Hypnotherapy

A collaborative, voluntary process.

You can ask questions, pause or decline any exercise. Experiences and outcomes vary. Discuss suitability before booking; sessions do not replace necessary medical or mental-health care.
