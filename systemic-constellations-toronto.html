Individual constellation work

Look at the relationships around a question.

A constellation is an experiential way to map a situation. We may use positions, objects or representations to explore how you perceive relationships, roles and competing needs. Your observations are starting points for reflection, not proof about other people or your family history.

01 / Focus

Bring a question

Choose a recurring relationship dynamic, a difficult decision or a role that feels uncomfortable.

02 / Map

Explore the system

Consider who or what matters in the situation, what feels missing and how the picture changes from another position.

03 / Reflect

Take it into daily life

Separate what you observed from what you know, and choose a conversation, boundary or next step to consider.

Family and relationship patterns

Is this an individual session or a group workshop?

How is it different from hypnosis?

Constellation work explores a situation through relationships and positions. Hypnosis involves guided attention and imagination. Discuss which approach fits your goal; you do not need to combine them.
