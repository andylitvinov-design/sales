## 1. Goal

The objective is not to maximize impressions or followers. The objective is to fill the remaining seats with appropriate participants by October 4 while preserving trust in the Toronto Tantra / Heart Healing community.

North-star metric:

**Paid confirmed registrations / remaining available seats.**

Define:

- C = total practical capacity of the room.
- P = currently paid + confirmed registrations.
- G = seat gap = C - P.
- Fill rate = P / C.

Every marketing decision this week should be judged by whether it reduces G.

Secondary metrics:

1. Eventbrite page views by tracking link.
2. Orders by tracking link.
3. Cost per paid registration from Eventbrite Ads.
4. Warm-DM reply rate.
5. Warm-DM paid registrations.
6. Promo-code usage.
7. Partner / Ola registrations.
8. Meetup registrations or clicks into Eventbrite.

Do not optimize for likes, comments, Reel views, or raw ad impressions unless they lead to Eventbrite page visits or registrations.

## 3. Positioning: What We Are Actually Selling

Do not primarily sell techniques.

The participant does not wake up wanting:

- "Part 1 Somatic Work"
- "Part 2 Systemic Principles"
- "Part 3 Paired Field Work"
- "Part 4 Archetypal Group Flow"

Those are delivery mechanisms.

Sell the experience:

**See and feel patterns around Love, Relationships and Money in a way that conversation alone may not reveal.**

### Primary positioning

**HEART HEALING: Love & Money — Family Constellations + Somatic Experience**

This keeps the Heart Healing identity while immediately explaining the modality.

Alternative if title length is constrained:

**Love & Money: Family Constellations + Somatic Experience**

Use "Temple", "Archetypal", "Field Work" and the deeper philosophy lower on the page after the visitor understands the basic offer.

### Primary promise

Use:

> Explore your patterns around Love, Relationships & Money through Systemic Family Constellations, somatic movement and guided group experiences.

Do not promise:

- healing finances,
- more money,
- curing trauma,
- treatment outcomes,
- guaranteed transformation.

Use words such as:

- explore,
- notice,
- experience,
- understand,
- reconnect,
- experiment,
- reflect,
- personal growth.

### Curiosity hook

> What happens when Love and Money become living figures in the room?

Secondary hook:

> We can understand our patterns intellectually for years. Constellation work gives us another way to see and experience them.

### Safety / friction remover

Near the top of every page or ad:

> Come alone. No previous experience is required. Your level of participation and contact is always your choice.

For cold audiences, this line is more valuable than adding more philosophy.

## 13. Paid Advertising Rules

### Eventbrite Ads

Current platform facts:

- Increase Awareness and Drive Traffic are available objectives.
- Boost Attendance is limited to free events.
- Default geo radius can be 80 km.
- Allowed local radius is approximately 16–80 km.

For this event:

- use Drive Traffic,
- use a Toronto-centered radius near the lower end,
- keep $10/day during the learning period,
- do not increase merely because the event is getting close.

### Scale rule

Scale only when a channel is showing evidence of orders.

Operating heuristic, not an industry benchmark:

- If a paid channel produces meaningful landing-page traffic but zero orders, stop treating reach as the problem.
- Diagnose first-screen message, trust, price, ticket structure and audience fit before buying more traffic.

### Meta

If launched:

- one campaign,
- one local audience structure,
- maximum two core creatives,
- $10–15/day starting budget,
- Eventbrite destination,
- Wednesday–Saturday initially.

Do not fragment a $10 budget across many ad sets.

## 14. Diagnosis Matrix

| Symptom | Likely issue | Action |
|---|---|---|
| Very few page visits | Distribution / creative | Improve hook, partner reach, Meetup, warm outreach |
| Many page visits, few/no orders | Page / trust / offer / price | Fix first screen, FAQs, facilitator credibility, ticket structure |
| DMs get replies but few purchases | Friction / price / uncertainty | Answer objections, simplify checkout, limited community option if justified |
| DMs get almost no replies | Wrong segment / generic copy | Narrow audience, personalize first line, change hook |

| Warm audience buys | Offer is valid | Add partner referrals; consider Meta |
| Ola/partner converts | Strong partner-market fit | Give partner more assets / reminders |
| Meetup gets traffic but no sales | Listing mismatch | Make date/location/offer explicit and direct to Eventbrite |
| Friday gap still high | Funnel issue, not automatically budget issue | Diagnose stage, referral push, limited community tickets |

## 15. Scarcity Rules

Use only true scarcity.

Allowed:

- "Registration closes tonight" if it actually does.
- "A few places remain" if true.
- "Community code ends Friday at 11:59 PM" if configured that way.
- "10 community tickets" if quantity is actually 10.

Avoid:

- "Almost sold out" when it is not.
- Fake countdowns.
- Fake attendee numbers.
- Repeated "last chance" for multiple days.

Trust is more valuable than one marginal ticket.
