Before you decide

What does “hypnotherapy” mean here?

Here it describes guided hypnosis and reflective work around personal goals and patterns. This offer does not promise diagnosis, medical treatment or a particular outcome.

Yes. Discuss the approach before starting, ask questions and choose whether to participate. You can ask to pause or stop at any time.
